Version: 1.0

# 1. Product Identity

FounderOS AI is an AI Chief of Staff designed for founder-led service businesses.

It acts as an intelligent executive partner that understands business context, prioritizes decisions, monitors operations, and recommends actions.

FounderOS AI is not another CRM or ERP.

It sits above existing business tools and becomes the founder's daily decision engine.

# 3. Business Problem

Founders spend their mornings switching between:

- Gmail
- WhatsApp
- CRM
- Calendar
- Excel
- Accounting
- Slack
- Meetings

Critical information is scattered.

Important decisions are delayed.

Business opportunities are missed.

FounderOS AI solves this fragmentation.
