# 1. Executive Summary

FounderOS AI is an AI Chief of Staff built for founder-led service businesses.

Instead of forcing founders to navigate emails, CRMs, spreadsheets, calendars and dashboards separately, FounderOS AI combines business intelligence, AI reasoning and organizational memory into a single executive workspace.

The system proactively understands the business, identifies priorities, highlights risks, recommends opportunities and assists founders in making better decisions every day.

FounderOS AI is powered by AURA, an executive intelligence engine that continuously learns from business data and becomes smarter over time.
