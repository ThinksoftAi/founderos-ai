Version: 1.0

# Executive Summary

FounderOS AI is an AI Chief of Staff designed for founder-led service businesses.

Its purpose is to help founders make faster, smarter business decisions every day through proactive executive intelligence.

The MVP focuses on solving one problem exceptionally well:

**Reducing decision fatigue for founders.**

# Core Problem

A founder starts the day by checking:

- Gmail
- Calendar
- WhatsApp
- CRM
- Excel

# MVP Modules

## 1. Executive Brief

Daily AI-generated business briefing.

Includes:

- Business Health
- Priorities
- Risks
- Opportunities
- Strategic Recommendation

---

## 2. AI Command Center

Natural language interface.

Examples:

"What should I focus on today?"

"Which client needs attention?"

"What are my biggest risks?"

---

## 3. Business Replay

AI-generated explanation of:

- Last 24 hours
- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days

Explains:

What happened.

Why it happened.

What should happen next.

---

## 4. Founder Memory

Persistent business memory.

Remembers

- Decisions
- Meetings
- Clients
- Goals
- Preferences
- Conversations

---

## 5. CRM Lite

Manage:

- Clients
- Leads
- Opportunities

Nothing more.

---

## 6. Tasks

Simple task management.

Priority

Due Date

Status

AI Suggestions

---

## 7. Gmail Integration

Read business emails.

Generate summaries.

Identify opportunities.

Detect risks.

---

## 8. Google Calendar

Understand founder schedule.

Prioritize meetings.

Prepare meeting briefings.
